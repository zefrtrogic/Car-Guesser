How to Play

  • A new car is chosen every day for everyone.
  • You get 5 guesses to identify the brand, model, and year.
  • The photo starts heavily zoomed in. Every wrong guess zooms it out a bit, giving you a better look.
  • Brand and model must match exactly (pick from the suggestions as you type).
  • For year, you'll get a hint: 🔼 too low, 🔽 too high, 🟡 within 3 years, 🟢 exact.
  • Guess correctly on brand, model, and year to win!
