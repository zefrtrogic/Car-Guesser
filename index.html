CAR GUESSER

CASE —

Unidentified vehicle sighted. You have 5 looks through the lens — each miss racks the zoom out.

Acquiring signal…
LENS 400mm STATUS: UNIDENTIFIED
SIGHTING 1 / 5

      How to spot it

      • One unidentified car is sighted per day — same car for everyone.
      • You get 5 sightings to log the make, model, and model year.
      • The lens starts racked way in. Every miss pulls focus back a stop, widening the shot.
      • Make and model must match exactly — type to pull up real matches, then lock one in.
      • Model year gets a read: ▲ too early, ▼ too late, an amber lock within 3 years, green on the nose.
      • Nail make, model, and year to close the case.
